# OLJ Copilot

## Context
A free Chrome extension for OnlineJobs.ph applications, and a side project of my own. Drafts use Google Gemini. The extension is hosted on Vercel. It is independent, and not affiliated with OnlineJobs.ph.

## Problem
An application needs the job post read, the subject line and apply points the employer asked for filled in, the post kept open beside the apply form, a message drafted from the applicant's resume on request, and the application tracked.

## Approach
- **Read and fill:** it reads the job post and fills in the subject line and apply points the employer asked for.
- **Side by side:** it keeps the post open beside the apply form.
- **Draft:** on request, it drafts a message from the applicant's resume with Google Gemini. The resume stays in the browser until upload or generate.
- **Send:** the user reviews and sends every message.
- **Track:** it tracks applications.
- **Host:** hosted on Vercel.

## Outcome
Free at [oljcopilot.vercel.app](https://oljcopilot.vercel.app/?utm_source=portfolio&utm_medium=referral). Independent, and not affiliated with OnlineJobs.ph.
